Legacy no-signature scans are labelled not-detected; user-directed legacy
classifications are labelled manual-override. Neither is verified absence.
Build binding remains unknown; no legacy app execution policy is exported.

The qualified count uses only current-feed products with explicit full,
non-bundle/non-preorder game SKU metadata, a past Store market listing date,
and known Windows.Desktop package metadata. It excludes ambiguous editions,
collections/trilogies, previews, launchers, demos and duplicate
title/group/content/package-family identities.
This is a conservative catalogue-rule count, not independent proof of worldwide
release, installation, playability, ownership or anti-cheat absence. Store dates
are listing dates for the observed market, not worldwide original launch dates.
Unknown and missing products remain explicit. Raw package formats are retained;
unrecognized formats do not silently become MSIXVC.

Only files listed in manifest-v1.json constitute the reusable candidate. Never
distribute the entire CompatibilityData directory: its other files include
internal QA and operational records. SHA256SUMS identifies bytes, not publisher
authentication. No independent updater or detached signature is implemented.
